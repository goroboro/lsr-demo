From **Tuesday 3 March 2026** we've been meeting every **Tuesday, 2.00–3.30pm**, at the Market Harborough library. New members are very welcome to join.

As always, it's free, there's no need to book, and there's no pressure to speak — just come and listen to some great stories and poems being read aloud. See [all our groups](../../groups.html) for the full list.
