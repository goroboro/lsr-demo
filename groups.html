Nine weekly groups

Our groups

We offer nine different groups, so you can enjoy shared reading in the comfort of your own home or in the company of others at your local library. Each group meets every week and is free and open to all. Just drop in — there’s no need to book, and no pressure to speak up.

A note for visitors from outside Leicestershire

Leicestershire Shared Reading is based in Leicester, Leicestershire & Rutland, but all are welcome to join us and experience the joy of shared reading before discovering whether other suitable reading groups are available closer to home. To find information about reading groups elsewhere in the UK, please see The Reader, the charity that pioneered the model we follow.

Can’t find a group near you?

Groups open and close as we find new volunteer leaders. Get in touch and tell us where you are — it might be the nudge we need to start a group in your area.
