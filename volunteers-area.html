For our volunteers

Volunteers’ area

A private corner of the site where our Reader Leaders and steering group members keep everything they need to run great groups — in one place, instead of across Dropbox and Google Drive.

Coming soon

This area is being redesigned

Volunteer resources are currently shared in our Google Drive folder. In the new site, this area will bring them all together behind a simple login, so volunteers can find what they need in one place.

Until then, if you’re a volunteer and can’t find the link you need, get in touch and we’ll point you in the right direction.

What will live here

Training

Read to Lead resources

Training materials, preparation guides, and the session-planning tools Reader Leaders use to choose and prepare their stories and poems.

Reports

Session reports

A shared home for the short report after every session — what was read, how it was received, and a log of material covered. Volunteers submit them via a short form, and they’re filed automatically.

Organisation

Running LSR

Steering group notes, meeting minutes, policies and contacts — everything that keeps the organisation running smoothly, versioned and easy to find.

Leicestershire
Shared Reading

A voluntary organisation running free, weekly ‘Make Friends, with a Book’ shared reading groups across Leicestershire, in partnership with the county’s library service.
