For everyone

Come to a group

Do you find it hard to pick a good book? Not read anything for ages? Come along to a group. We meet every week and share a story and some poems — there’s no homework and nothing to prepare. It’s free, friendly and light-hearted, and you can come as often or as little as you like.

The heart of LSR

Volunteer role: Reader Leader

Bring great literature to life through the practice of shared reading! LSR follows the shared reading model developed by The Reader, a charity and social enterprise. The model uses a range of great literature to encourage the sharing of thoughts and experiences, allowing personal and social connections to be made. It’s not about teaching literature — there is no academic content in what we do.

Leading a group involves

  • Selecting and preparing literature to read
  • Managing the running of a group meeting
  • Reading aloud with the group
  • Taking the lead in discussions that help participants connect the literature to their own lives
  • Keeping and reporting a brief record of each session, and passing on any concerns to your mentor
  • For Zoom groups: being confident with the technology, e.g. screen-sharing and setting up meetings

As part of the LSR team you will also

  • Take part in ongoing training and personal development, including mentoring and reflection on your practice
  • Stay in touch with fellow group leaders by email, at support meetings and social gatherings

Time commitment

Groups meet weekly, and you and your co-leaders will arrange how these are shared out — expect to lead up to one session a fortnight. Sessions last 1.5 hours, and you’ll also allow time for training, preparation and support meetings.

Personally, you’ll enjoy developing new literary and communication skills, meeting new people, and engaging with your local community. The Read to Lead course — a series of invigorating, thought-provoking sessions over six half-days — is the start of it all.

Is this you?

  • You enjoy reading for pleasure and believe great literature can touch and change lives
  • You’re an excellent reader with good spoken English, confident reading aloud
  • You’re a people person — kind, tolerant and engaging, able to put others first
  • You can commit to preparing and running a session every fortnight, apart from holidays
  • You can attend the online Read to Lead training course, at a time to be agreed
Five easy steps

How to apply for Reader Leader training

  1. Apply to volunteer

    Complete our online application form: Leicestershire Shared Reading — Reader Leader volunteer.

  2. We chat

    Our admin volunteer will contact you to arrange a time to discuss the role, and will introduce you to a current Reader Leader who will invite you to attend some sessions so you can experience them for yourself.

  3. An informal interview

    After attending a few sessions, if you’d like to become a Reader Leader we’ll arrange an informal interview.

  4. Complete the training course form

    Following the interview, if everyone is happy to proceed, you’ll be asked to complete the Read to Lead training course form.

  5. Read to Lead

    You’ll take part in the online Read to Lead training course, which runs over six half-day sessions.

Not quite ready for the full role? Email us for an informal chat and we’ll help you discover if this is you.

Steering group

Volunteer behind the scenes

Steering group members work together under the direction of the Chair to ensure the smooth running of LSR. You’ll be allocated a portfolio of responsibilities, broadly covered by one of these roles: Group Secretary, Volunteer Leader, Information Leader, Publicity & Marketing Leader, or Fundraiser. In addition, all members are expected to attend up to four committee meetings and the AGM each year; otherwise the time commitment is flexible. The role is voluntary, with reimbursement of reasonable expenses.
