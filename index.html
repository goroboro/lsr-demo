Who we are

What is Leicestershire Shared Reading?

Leicestershire Shared Reading (LSR) is a voluntary organisation, set up in 2016, working in partnership with Leicestershire County Council’s library service to support a thriving network of ‘Make Friends, with a Book’ shared reading groups.

Our groups, led by volunteer Reader Leaders, meet weekly in person at public libraries around Leicestershire and online via Zoom. They are open to anyone — just drop in and sit for a while, listening to stories and poems being read aloud.

What happens in a group?

Every week a volunteer Reader Leader brings a story and a poem to share. Here’s the rhythm of a session:

Read aloud

A story and a poem, read together

The Reader Leader — or a willing member — reads aloud from great literature. Everyone has a copy of the text to follow along. We never ask anyone to read unless they’d like to.

Pause & reflect

We stop and talk about it

Throughout the session the group pauses to work out what’s going on together, sharing thoughts and making personal connections with the literature.

Connect

A safe space, for everyone

The Reader Leader encourages discussion and creates a space where members feel valued, encouraged and listened to. It’s fine to just listen.

The reading list

What do we read?

Choosing ‘good’ literature is key to the success of shared reading. Not everything we read is serious, but it all has depth and makes us think and feel. Discussions tend to focus on characters and what makes them tick rather than plot — and we enjoy finding out what a poem means.

We usually read a short story and a poem or two in each session, though some groups prefer to work through a novel alongside the poetry. Members often talk about the delight of getting to know new writers, as well as the pleasure of revisiting familiar works.

Julia Barnes Amy Tan Italo Calvino Andrea Levy Elizabeth Gaskell Seamus Heaney Mark Doty Roger McGough Carol Ann Duffy …and plenty more
