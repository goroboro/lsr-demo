In early October, Nicky and Helena donned their glad rags to attend the 2026 VAL Volunteer Awards ceremony at Winstanley House in Leicester. Our invitation came about after LSR reached the finals in the **Volunteer-Led Organisation of the Year** category. We are proud that our volunteers have received this recognition — every one of us gives their time and works with dedication and commitment to enrich the lives of people in our communities.

This annual event celebrates the contribution and achievements of individuals and organisations from the Voluntary, Community and Social Enterprise sector in Leicester and Leicestershire. It's a huge field — we learned that as well as around 2,400 registered charities in the area there are roughly 3,800 grassroots community organisations like ours. For our organisation to reach the final four in our category was therefore especially pleasing!
